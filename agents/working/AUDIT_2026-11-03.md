# Audit, 2026-11-03

A read-only audit of the whole repository at `388aa13` (PRADO `master` after the PHP 8.2 and
ActiveRecord split). Six areas were reviewed in parallel: consent core, regions and IP lookup, the
UI controls, data subject rights, retention and the database layer, and docs, packaging and CI.
The findings in the summary table were checked against the code, and the framework where it
mattered. Legal readings are marked where they are a judgement rather than a code fact.

## Status

Fixed since the audit: 1, 2, 3, 4, 8 (partial `setConsent()`; `api` grants still lift GPC by
design, per CCPA regulations §7025(c)(3)), 9 and 10. An expired opt-out still ends when the cookie
does (`Expires`, at most 395 days); a longer life for opt-outs would need a store change.

## Consent core

1. **High: opt-out and none regions re-grant on Version change, expiry or withdrawal.**
   `src/Consent/TConsentModule.php:259` (no record → `Granted` outside opt-in) with `getRecord()`
   `:404` and `withdraw()` `:372`. A Californian who chose Reject all (a CCPA opt-out of sale and
   sharing) is back to `marketing=granted` after a `Version` change, 180 days, or an erasure
   request (`TConsentPersonalDataProvider` calls `withdraw()`); `onConsentChanged` then pushes the
   grant to adapters. The CCPA regulations (§7026) expect an opt-out to stand and not be re-asked
   for 12 months. Fix: outside opt-in, keep honouring the denials of a stale or expired record,
   and make `withdraw()` store an all-denied record instead of clearing.
8. **Medium: `setConsent()` carries the raw stored state for omitted categories.** `:250, 256, 323`.
   With GPC on and a `banner` record holding `marketing=granted`, `setConsent(['analytics'=>true])`
   writes `marketing=granted` with source `api`, which counts as explicit and lifts GPC. The README
   (`:162`) says only a preferences grant overrides GPC. Fix: carry effective state for omitted
   categories and drop `api` from the explicit sources, or document it.
9. **Medium: the withdrawal log contradicts effective consent.** `:377-385` logs every category
   `denied`; outside opt-in the effective state right after is `granted`. Fixed with 1.
10. **Medium: `TDbConsentStore` restores a withdrawn subject.** `src/Consent/TDbConsentStore.php:81-88,
    137-141`: `clear()` deletes only the current consent id; the next request restores the subject's
    newest other row (another device). Fix: clear or tombstone all of the subject's rows.
- Low: consent cookies are signed with `hashData()` (ValidationKey), not 4.4's `hashCookieData()`
  (CookieValidationKey), so rotating the cookie key leaves them valid. `TCookieConsentStore.php:146,162`.
- Low: `TPrivacyHash` uses ValidationKey with the same HMAC as `hashData()`; no exploit found, but
  a derived key is cleaner. README should say to set ValidationKey on multi-node deployments.
- Low: on a shared device, `TDbConsentStore::save()` re-tags another user's row to whoever is
  logged in (`:67`), hiding it from the first user's access and erasure requests.
- Low: `reset()` does not re-read the store; `TCookieConsentStore::load()` keeps its cache (`:499`).
- Low: a category added without a Version bump is never asked (`:293`).
- Low: `dyConsent` output is not re-validated (a behavior can deny `necessary` or drop keys).
- Low: `$source` is unvalidated against `VARCHAR(16)`; on strict MySQL the store saves but the log
  insert fails.
- Low, docs: `TConsentStateRecord` says `granted`/`denied` but `1`/`0` is stored; the design's
  `gpc-optout` source and `dyConsent($consent, $module)` signature do not exist; lowering `Expires`
  does not shorten existing records.

Solid: tamper-proof cookies (expiry, version and source signed; `hash_equals`; strict JSON
parsing), required categories forced on read and write, unknown region → opt-in, parameterised
SQL, keyed hashes, no session started on read.

## Regions and IP lookup

3. **Medium: EU outermost regions and Åland missing.** `src/Consent/Region/TRegionGroups.php:45`.
   GeoIP returns `RE`, `GP`, `MQ`, `GF`, `YT`, `MF`, `AX` for them; they resolve to mode `none`, so no
   banner and every category granted, though GDPR and ePrivacy apply (Art. 355(1) TFEU). Do not
   add `BL`, which left the EU in 2012.
4. **Medium: country-only US answers are mode `none`.** `TRegionGroups::matches('US', [...'US-CA'])`
   is false. With `CF-IPCountry: US` (no subdivision header) or the country-only `ipinfo-lite`
   preset, `HonorGpc="required"` ignores a Californian's GPC. Fix: treat a country that has
   subdivisions in `OptOutFor` as opt-out when the subdivision is unknown, and let the chain go on
   to the next resolver.
5. **Medium: lookup exceptions are not contained.** `TIpRegionResolver.php:105-111`,
   `TMaxMindDbLocator.php:81-89`. A missing `.mmdb` at runtime, `InvalidDatabaseException`, a custom
   locator or a cache error breaks every page; design §8.1 says failures are never thrown to the
   page. Fix: catch `\Throwable` around the lookup, log a warning, return null (opt-in).
6. **Medium: IPv4-mapped IPv6.** `inRange('::ffff:10.0.0.5', '10.0.0.0/8')` is false, and
   `filter_var` with `NO_PRIV|NO_RES` accepts `::ffff:10.0.0.1`. On a dual-stack listener the load
   balancer is not trusted, its private address goes to the API, and every visitor is unknown.
   Fix: unmap `::ffff:0:0/96` to IPv4 before matching and validation.
7. **Medium: HTTP transport.** `THttpTransportTrait.php:35-47`: `follow_location` is on (a redirect
   re-sends `AuthHeader` and the visitor IP, possibly to `http://`), `timeout` is per read not
   total, and `stream_get_contents` is unbounded. Fix: no redirects, a total deadline, read at
   most ~64 KB.
- Low/medium, legal judgement: `US-GPC` likely lacks `US-NE` (Nebraska Data Privacy Act, in force
  2025-01-01, requires honouring universal opt-out). Check against the statute before adding.
- Low: `Forwarded` (RFC 7239) is documented in the design but not read, and `X-Forwarded-For`
  hops with a port fail validation (fails closed).
- Low: `TMaxMindDbLocator.php:67` logs the visitor's IP in clear.
- Low: two resolvers on one cache share keys (no module id or locator in the key).
- Low: the `ipstack` preset uses https, which its free plan lacks (per design §8.1).

Solid: rightmost-untrusted `X-Forwarded-For` selection, IP validated before URL substitution,
`rawurlencode` on keys, no key in logs, TLS verification on, HMAC cache keys, fail-closed region
normalization, exact `Sec-GPC: 1` parsing, correct IPv4/IPv6 CIDR math, the third-party lookup is
opt-in and documented.

## UI controls

2. **Medium: the gate pre-renders denied content.** `src/Consent/Controls/TConsentGate.php:95-106`
   only skips rendering; the children stay visible, so `TControl::preRenderRecursive` runs their
   `onPreRender` and data binding. A tracker or map control inside a denied gate that registers a
   head script emits `<script src="https://third-party…">` without consent, contradicting the class
   docblock. Fix: make the content children invisible (or wrap them) before pre-render when not
   granted; add a unit test with a child that registers a head script.
11. **Medium: Customize and the link need a preferences control on the page.**
    `TConsentBanner.php:114,142`, `TConsentLink.php:93-97` only set `PreferencesOpen`. On a page
    without `TConsentPreferences`, the link's callback prevents its `PreferencesUrl` href and
    nothing opens, so withdrawal (Art. 7(3)) is unreachable there; Customize hides the banner and
    shows nothing. Fix: fall back to a plain link / redirect when no preferences control is
    registered, and keep the banner shown.
12. **Medium, accessibility: no focus management.** Each callback replaces the `TActivePanel`, so
    focus drops to `<body>` after Accept/Reject/Save/Close, and Customize does not move focus into
    the preferences. Fix: a nonce'd callback script that focuses the preferences heading on open
    and returns focus on close; add a Playwright keyboard test.
- Medium, accessibility (plausible): dark mode gives the policy link no colour; default `#0000EE`
  on `#1f1f1f` is ~1.75:1. Set link colours and `color-scheme: light dark`. `assets/consent.css:74-92`.
- Low, XSS hardening: category titles render raw (`TCheckBox` encodes only with `Encode=true`,
  default false on master) at `TConsentPreferences.php:87`; `TConsentLink` text likewise; no
  scheme check on `PolicyUrl`/`PreferencesUrl` (`javascript:` passes). All developer-configured.
- Low: Accept all / Reject all inside the preferences log source `banner`, not `preferences`
  (`TConsentPreferences.php:165,177`), which also changes GPC precedence.
- Low, accessibility (plausible): the fixed banner can cover a focused footer link (WCAG 2.4.11).
- Test gaps: gated head scripts, encoding of `<`/`&` in titles and link text, link/customize
  without preferences, source of preferences accept/reject, keyboard/focus/axe, dark-mode
  prominence, CSRF rejection.

Solid: CSRF validation on for postbacks and callbacks; callback scripts use the CSP nonce; banner
texts encoded; Reject and Accept equally prominent on the first layer; no dismiss-without-choice;
non-necessary boxes unchecked while undecided; postback and callback paths share the module calls
and are both tested end to end.

## Data subject rights

13. **Medium: partial exports look complete.** `src/Rights/TPrivacyModule.php:209-221` returns no
    status; a provider that threw is simply absent from `data`. Fix: add `status` and per-provider
    errors to the export.
14. **Medium: soft failures count as success.** `:294-307`, `:270-273`: a `TErasureResult` with
    `addError()`, or `rectifyPersonalData()` returning false (always, for `consent`), is
    `completed`.
15. **Medium: opened requests cannot be closed.** `export()`/`erase()`/`rectify()` always call
    `openRequest()`; the README's record-now-answer-later pattern leaves a `pending` row forever
    plus a second row. Fix: accept an existing request id (and `--request=` in the shell).
16. **Medium: `failed` and `partial` are never overdue.** `getOverdueRequests()` reads only
    `pending`.
- Low/medium: the due date is `ResponseDays × 86400`, not a calendar month (GDPR Art. 12(3)); no
  extension API; one value for GDPR and CCPA.
- Low: `exportJson()` encodes after the request is logged `completed`; invalid UTF-8 throws.
- Low: request-log retention purges unanswered requests (`TDbPrivacyRequestLog.php:108-111`).
- Low: `privacy/erase` has no existence check, confirmation or dry run; the shell cannot pass
  email or identifiers.
- Low: provider exception messages (which may hold personal data) go to the application log.
- Low: the export omits the subject's own request history.
- Low: rectify drops a provider that threw from its result instead of marking it false.
- Low: request rows link on the user name only; a rename or a ValidationKey rotation breaks
  `getRequests()`. Document it.
- Plausible: the README lists the session provider before the consent provider; with
  `TSessionConsentStore` the consent withdrawal writes to a destroyed session.

Solid: verification documented as the application's job everywhere; self-scoped providers gate on
the current user; the request log stores a hash, counts and class names only; consent-log rows
retained with an Art. 7(1)/17(3)(b) reason; a throwing provider does not stop the others; JSON-only
export; user state unserialized with `allowed_classes => false`.

## Retention and the database layer

17. **Medium: purge failures are invisible to cron.** `TRetentionModule.php:179-194` catches
    `\Throwable` per policy and returns normally; `TCronMethodTask` passes no cancellation token,
    so `TaskTimeout` cannot stop a long purge, and blocking PDO starves other fibers. Fix: throw
    after `onPurged` when a policy failed, rethrow cancellation, offer a token-aware task.
18. **Medium: DDL on every process.** `TPrivacySchema.php:145-156`: with `AutoCreateTables` on (the
    default), each process runs `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` (and indexes) on first use. On MySQL
    with a shared connection this implicitly commits an open application transaction, a
    least-privilege user fails on every request, and it takes metadata locks. Fix: check existence
    first; document `AutoCreateTables="false"` for production.
19. **Medium: unbatched delete.** `TDbRetentionPurger.php:65-69` (and the built-in logs) delete in
    one statement. Fix: an optional `BatchSize` loop.
20. **Medium: `P0D` docs.** `TRetentionPolicy.php:24-25`: for a `created_at` purger, `P0D` deletes
    the whole table. Fix the docblock; consider refusing `P0D` for `TDbRetentionPurger`.
- Low/medium, design vs code: the design keeps consent-log rows "3 years after the last change for
  the consent id"; `TDbConsentLog::purge()` deletes each row by its own `created_at`.
- Low: `TimeFormat="datetime"` binds UTC but compares in the DB session's time zone; ISO `T`
  strings and millisecond epochs mis-compare.
- Low: identifiers are validated but not quoted (reserved words, mixed-case pgsql tables fail).
- Low: the Art. 30 record shows the policy period instead of `RetentionPeriod` (consent-choice
  reads "P0D…"), and shows "-" without warning when no policy resolves.
- Low: no joint-controller or Art. 27 representative fields; no Art. 30(2) processor records.
- Low (plausible): the created-tables cache is keyed by `spl_object_id`, reused after GC in
  long-running workers; use a `WeakMap`.
- Low: `requested_by` is not truncated to `VARCHAR(255)`.

Solid: identifier validation blocks injection; calendar-aware UTC cutoffs on the clock seam; the
DDL is consistent across sqlite/mysql/pgsql and matches `config/schema/*.sql`; `TLegalBasis`
covers Art. 6(1)(a)–(f); `TProcessingActivity` covers Art. 30(1)(b)–(g); every built-in store
declares its activity and retention.
